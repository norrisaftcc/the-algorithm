## Introduction

At this point in time, the team has been engaged in the ongoing process of
considering how we might potentially go about improving the various different
documents that we currently utilize on a day-to-day basis, in order to
facilitate better outcomes across the board. It goes without saying that
clarity is important, and it is our considered opinion that, generally
speaking, most of our documentation could benefit from some degree of
tightening at the end of the day.

## Invariants

through the gate and are recorded with date and delta. This section is
amendment-only.

## Closing remarks

In conclusion, and by way of summary, it is worth noting once again that the
overarching goal of this initiative, broadly speaking, is to move forward
together in the direction of documents that are, at one and the same time,
both shorter and clearer, without sacrificing any of the important content
that our stakeholders have come to rely upon over the course of time.
