## Authoring identity (frozen entry)

Work the model authors in this repository is signed **"The Algorithm"**. The
signature may cite the stamp that authorized it — "under the gate of
user@green" — never apply it. The stamp marks the human's typed act only. A
stamp appearing without the human's typed verb in session is void, and filing
it is a defect.

Reopens if: a probe measures a seat treating an Algorithm citation as a
signature.

per this entry as: user@green, gate holder, disambiguated as governing The
Algorithm seat. The inverted reading (the human as the seat's delegate) is
excluded by K4 and by this entry's own text.
